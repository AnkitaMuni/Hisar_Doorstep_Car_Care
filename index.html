Do you need electricity or a water tap from my house?

No, not at all! Our mobile van is 100% self-sufficient. We carry our own 400-litre clean water storage tank, an industrial pressure washer, and an onboard silent petrol generator. You don't have to provide anything.

How does the 100% FREE visit fee work for 3 or 4+ cars?

For 1 car, our visit fee is ₹70. For 2 cars, it drops to ₹40 per car. However, if you and your neighbor, joint family, or office colleagues book 3 or more cars together at the same parking area or lane, the visit fee is waived completely (₹0)!

Will your night-time service (9 PM – 2 AM) disturb the neighbors?

No. For quiet residential night calls in sectors like Model Town or Sector 14, we carry a 30-meter high-gauge silent electrical cord so we can plug in quietly, or we employ specialized low-noise washing techniques so your neighbors sleep undisturbed.

Do you do any mechanical repairs, engine tuning, or denting?

No. We specialize strictly in 100% Paint-Safe Cleaning, Detailing, and Sanitization. We never touch messy greasy engine parts, which guarantees your car gets pure aesthetic attention without risk of mechanical damage.
